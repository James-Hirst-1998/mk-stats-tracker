# Who a blue shell was after

A blue shell locks on to whoever is leading about two seconds before it comes
down. The dashboard now works that out, so each blast says who it got and who
was collateral, only standing next to them. A dodge only counts when it locked on
to you and missed; a shell that hit nobody while the lead was changing hands
is nobody's dodge. Launches nobody threw, like the cannonballs on Shy Guy
Beach and the Bob-omb cars on Moonview, are put down to the course.
